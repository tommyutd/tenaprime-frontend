About Our Gym

TenaPrime Fitness Club is Addis Ababa's premier fitness destination, offering state-of-the-art equipment, expert trainers, and a supportive community environment to help you achieve your fitness goals.

Established in 2020, we've quickly become the city's favorite place to train, with members experiencing real results through our personalized approach to fitness.
